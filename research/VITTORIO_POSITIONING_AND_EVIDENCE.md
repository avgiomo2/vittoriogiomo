# Vittorio Giomo — authority-first website concept
Date: 2026-11-26
Branch: `concept/vittorio-authority` (does not affect the live `main` deployment)

## Editorial position

Present Vittorio as an independent international authority in colour and textile intelligence, **not** merely a designer with a sample collection. Benchmark the positioning against Li Edelkoort / Trend Union, but avoid suggesting endorsement or a currently formal partnership.

### Lead message
**Colour is never just colour.**  
Five decades anticipating what colour and materials will mean next.

### Proposed structure
1. Monumental editorial hero + locked six-colour split flap
2. The Practice — colour intelligence, material thinking, ideas & impact
3. International voice — influential networks and institutional contributions
4. History — verified milestones and a growing archive of evidence
5. TESSUTECA — related but independently deployed archive platform
6. Future additions — original essays, lecture chronology, press, selected projects, recorded interviews, and inquiry form with real contact details

## Comparator lessons

**Li Edelkoort / Trend Union:** personal intellectual authority, research-led books, speaking and consultancy. https://www.edelkoort.us/our-story ; https://www.edelkoort.us/trend-tools

**Color Coloris:** credibility of organisations, forecasting as collective international dialogue. https://www.colorcoloris.com/team/

**Monolith NYC:** typographic scale, negative space, sparse elegant cataloguing. https://monolith.nyc/

**TESSUTECA:** credible material and archival depth; avoid marketing an unbuilt subscription platform as a working service.

## Design decisions

Retain approved high-contrast typography and six-colour sequential flap/timing. Keep the warm ivory palette and red accent. Mobile first at 320, 375, 390, 430, 768 px; body copy never pale on ivory, navigation must wrap, oversized typography must not overflow, animations must honour reduced motion. Introduce original work/images only after rights are clear. Avoid borrowed trend-book imagery on the live website.
