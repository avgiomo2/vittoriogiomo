## Research workflow
Check URLs and editorial updates monthly; add a dated changelog, screen/mobile observations, public pricing when verifiable, and concrete implications for Vittorio Giomo or TESSUTECA. Avoid representing approximate screen RGB swatches as Pantone-certified values.
